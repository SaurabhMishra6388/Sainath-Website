Who We Are

A production house built for every listener

Sainath International Entertainment is a production and marketing company for films and the wider entertainment industry. We produce songs across genres and are positioning ourselves as one of the best upcoming entertainment companies — for everyone who loves to be entertained.

From tender family dramas to Sai bhajans, sufi qawwalis and festival anthems, every release is made to find a proper place in the hearts of our audience.
